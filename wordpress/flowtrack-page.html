Start with one small step

  1. Choose your currency. Set the currency you use day to day before adding your records.
  2. Add something you earned or spent. Enter the amount, date, and description. Tags are optional.
  3. Build the habit. Check your monthly overview and download regular backups from Settings.

Private by design. Yours to look after.

The FlowTrack app sends no transaction records to us. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or cloud sync. Your records are stored in your browser, without encryption. Anyone with access to that browser profile can read them.

Back up regularly. Clearing browser data, using private browsing, losing your device, or switching browsers can leave you without your records. Download a JSON backup from Settings and keep it somewhere safe. CSV and JSON downloads contain readable financial data.

A few things to know

Can I use it offline?

Yes, after a successful first visit online and offline setup. Check Settings for offline readiness. Supported browsers can also install FlowTrack on your home screen. A first visit without internet will not work.

Does changing the currency convert my money?

No. The currency setting changes the label on the entire ledger; it does not apply exchange rates. Use one currency per ledger.

Can I move my records to another device?

Download a JSON backup on the old device, transfer it privately, and restore it in FlowTrack on the new device. Restore replaces that browser's existing ledger. There is no automatic sync.

Is it really free?

Yes. FlowTrack is free to use and its source is available under the MIT license. You can explore the code, suggest improvements, or adapt it for your own project.
