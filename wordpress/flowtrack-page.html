Privacy & backups

First use asks you to create a PIN. Compatible devices can use face recognition, fingerprint or a device PIN for quick unlock. Records stay in this browser. There is no cloud sync. Save a recovery key in the app to reset a forgotten PIN without losing records. Without a PIN, a recovery key saved beforehand, or a separate backup, records cannot be recovered. Clearing site data, private browsing or losing your device can erase your records. Download regular JSON backups in Settings to move between devices. JSON and CSV exports are unencrypted; keep them private.

Offline use needs a successful first online visit; check readiness in Settings. Currency selection changes labels only, with no conversion. FlowTrack sends no transaction records to us.
